A REMARKABLE LIFE CYCLE

Immortal Jellyfish

Turritopsis dohrnii is a tiny jellyfish known for an unusual ability: under certain conditions, it can transform from its adult stage back into an earlier polyp stage. This life-cycle reversal is rare and scientifically fascinating.

Survival & behaviour

The name “immortal jellyfish” can be misleading. It can still be eaten, injured, or killed by disease and environmental changes. Its remarkable trait is the ability to restart part of its life cycle, not invulnerability.

Creature facts
