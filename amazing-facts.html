Amazing Ocean Facts

Small discoveries, huge wonders. Explore surprising facts about the ocean, from glowing animals to the pressure of the deepest trenches.

~71%

One connected ocean

The ocean covers about 71% of Earth’s surface and helps regulate our climate.

10 m

Pressure rises fast

For every 10 metres of depth, water pressure increases by roughly one atmosphere.

Bioluminescence

Living lights

Many ocean organisms produce light through chemical reactions. Scientists call this bioluminescence.

Had al zone

Life under extremes

Some animals survive in deep trenches where it is cold, dark and pressure is immense.

Why is the ocean blue?

Water absorbs longer red wavelengths more strongly than blue wavelengths, so blue light is more likely to be scattered back to our eyes in many ocean conditions.

Ocean sound travels far

Sound can travel long distances underwater, which is why researchers use acoustic tools to study ocean environments.

Remember this

The deep sea is not empty: it is a complex habitat full of life adapted to darkness, cold and pressure.
