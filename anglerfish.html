THE LIGHT IN THE DARK

Anglerfish

Anglerfish live in deep waters where sunlight is scarce. Many females have a modified fin spine that works like a fishing rod, with a glowing lure that attracts prey close enough to catch.

Survival & behaviour

Anglerfish are ambush predators. Their large mouths and sharp teeth help them seize prey, and their expandable stomachs help them make use of meals that may be rare.

Creature facts

Special featureA lure that attracts prey
HabitatDeep, dim ocean waters; depth varies by species
DietFish and other small marine animals
SurvivalA large mouth helps capture passing prey
DEEP-SEA FACTSome deep-sea anglerfish make light with the help of bioluminescent bacteria living inside their lure. Not all anglerfish species live in the deepest ocean or use light in the same way.
