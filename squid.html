THE ELUSIVE DEEP-SEA GIANT

Giant Squid

The giant squid is a real deep-ocean cephalopod with eight arms and two longer feeding tentacles. It has enormous eyes that help gather the limited light in its environment, making it well suited to spotting movement in dim water.

Survival & behaviour

The two long tentacles help it seize prey and bring it toward its beak. Giant squid are thought to eat fish and other squid, although their behaviour is difficult to observe because they live far below the surface.

Creature facts

Scientific nameArchiteuthis dux
BodyEight arms and two long feeding tentacles
Special senseVery large eyes for low-light conditions
DietFish and other squid are believed to be part of its diet
DEEP-SEA FACTFor many years, scientists mostly studied specimens found dead or washed ashore. Deep-sea cameras have now recorded living giant squid in their natural habitat, helping researchers learn more about this elusive animal.
